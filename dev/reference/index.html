PhaseSpaceDTFE.PhaseSpaceDTFE — Module
PhaseSpaceDTFE

The density, velocity and number-of-streams fields of a three-dimensional N-body simulation, estimated with the Phase-Space Delaunay Tessellation Field Estimator (Feldbrugge 2024, MNRAS 536, 807, https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/536/1/807/7915986) on a tessellation of the initial (Lagrangian) particle positions: the periodic Delaunay tessellation, or the Kuhn split of the particle lattice.

source
PhaseSpaceDTFE.PS_DTFE — Type
PS_DTFE

Phase-space DTFE estimator on a tessellation of the Lagrangian particle distribution: the periodic Delaunay tessellation of the initial positions (default) or the Kuhn tetrahedralisation of the lattice. Every tetrahedron is stored once, in the Morton order of its Eulerian centroid. Its properties are

simplices    # (T, 4) particle rows (as in the input) of the corners of every tetrahedron
positions    # (N, 3) final positions, rows as in the input
geometry     # (12, T) first corner (at its image in [0, L)³) and inverse edge matrix (column-major, NaN for a
             # degenerate tetrahedron); empty for `compact = true`, where they are recomputed from the positions

Fields are interpolated linearly with the barycentric coordinates λ = B d of a point in each tetrahedron that contains it, where d is the separation of the point from the first corner, and summed over the streams. How a point and a tetrahedron are related depends on images:

  • :all (periodic estimator, PS_DTFE_periodic): the point is taken modulo the box and its separation from the first corner at the nearest periodic image, so that every tetrahedron is found at all its periodic images.
  • :modulo (wrapped sub-box estimator): the point is taken modulo the box and the tetrahedra are taken where they are stored; the periodic images are stored as separate tetrahedra (a frame around the box).
  • :none (non-periodic estimator, PS_DTFE_nonperiodic): the point and the tetrahedra are taken as they are.

estimator.periodicity is true unless images == :none.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.StreamVelocityGrid — Type
StreamVelocityGrid

Velocities of all streams on the regular grid xs × ys × zs, stored compactly since the number of streams differs between grid points:

nstreams::Array{Int32, 3}    # number of streams at every grid point [ix, iy, iz]
offsets::Vector{Int}         # the streams of the grid point with linear index I are rows offsets[I]:offsets[I + 1] - 1
velocity::Matrix{T}          # (S, 3): velocity of every stream
density::Vector{T}           # (S,): density of every stream
divergence::Vector{T}        # (S,): ∇·v of every stream                       (empty without `deformation`)
shear::Matrix{T}             # (S, 6): shear tensor σ, components xx yy zz xy xz yz   (empty without `deformation`)
vorticity::Matrix{T}         # (S, 3): vorticity ω = ∇×v                        (empty without `deformation`)

G[ix, iy, iz] is the (n, 3) view of the velocities of the n streams at the grid point, as velocity([x, y, z], estimator) (the streams in no particular order), and stream_density(G, ix, iy, iz) their densities. With deformation = true, stream_divergence, stream_shear and stream_vorticity give the velocity deformation of every stream, as divergence, shear and vorticity; stream_average gives density-weighted means over the streams. Construct it with velocity_grid or velocity_grid_sb.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.PS_DTFE_nonperiodic — Method
PS_DTFE_nonperiodic(coords_q, coords_x, velocities, m, sim_box; vertex_density = :mass, G, jitter, drop_simplices = true, compact = false)
PS_DTFE_nonperiodic(coords_q, coords_x, m, sim_box; ...)

Construct the phase-space DTFE estimator without periodic boundaries, from the Delaunay tessellation of the initial positions coords_q (only tessellation = :delaunay). The arguments are as for PS_DTFE_periodic; points are taken as given (images = :none), not modulo the box.

  • The final positions are unwrapped to the periodic image nearest to the initial positions: every displacement coords_x - coords_q is taken in [-L/2, L/2]. This suits particles of a periodic simulation whose positions are wrapped into the box. A particle that truly moves more than L/2 along an axis is placed at the wrong image, L away.
  • With drop_simplices = true (default) the tetrahedra with two corners more than three lattice spacings L/Ni apart along an axis are removed: the thin slivers on the convex hull of the initial positions.
  • The tetrahedra cover the convex hull of the particles, which lies inside the box: points between the hull and the faces of the box lie in no tetrahedron and have density zero.
  • Near the hull the stars of the particles are incomplete. With vertex_density = :mass this biases the vertex densities high there (for a uniform glass the density within 1.5 spacings of a face exceeds the mean by about a third); vertex_density = :lagrangian avoids this, with the mean density ρ̄ = M / Σ V_L of the tetrahedra kept.
  • Both vertex densities conserve the total mass, and :lagrangian is exact for affine maps in the interior.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.PS_DTFE_periodic — Method
PS_DTFE_periodic(coords_q, coords_x, velocities, m, sim_box; tessellation = :delaunay, vertex_density = :mass, G, pad, jitter, compact)
PS_DTFE_periodic(coords_q, coords_x, m, sim_box; ...)

Construct the phase-space DTFE estimator from the initial positions coords_q, the final positions coords_x and (optionally) the velocities of an N-body simulation in a periodic box. These are (N, 3) matrices with one row per particle, in the same order. m is the particle mass: a number, or a vector with the mass of every particle for simulations whose particle masses differ.

  • tessellation = :kuhn: the Kuhn split of the Lagrangian lattice, for lattice initial conditions with N = Ni³; the lattice site of every particle is recovered from coords_q, so the rows may be in any order.
  • tessellation = :delaunay (default): the periodic Delaunay tessellation of coords_q, as in PhaseSpaceDTFE; for glass or other non-lattice initial conditions. pad is the width of the frame of periodic images in units of L (default: four mean particle spacings); jitter (default 1e-5 mean spacings) breaks the degeneracy of lattice-like initial positions consistently across periodic images. With jitter = 0 the connectivity is exactly the Delaunay tessellation of coords_q, as in PhaseSpaceDTFE, which requires positions in general position (not an exact lattice).
  • vertex_density = :mass (default, as in PhaseSpaceDTFE) or :lagrangian; see vertex_densities. :lagrangian is recommended with :delaunay.
  • G sets the number of cells per side of the search index (default sim_box.Ni).
  • slabs sets the number of slabs of the periodic Delaunay tessellation that are tetrahedralised concurrently (default: one per thread); see periodic_delaunay.
  • compact = true does not store the first corner and inverse edge matrix of every tetrahedron, but recomputes them from the positions at every query, with identical results: the estimator needs about a third of the memory, and point queries are slower.

The forms with a depth argument after m, as in PhaseSpaceDTFE, are accepted; the depth is not used.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.ps_dtfe_grid — Method
ps_dtfe_grid(coords_q, coords_x, m, sim_box, xs, ys, zs; tessellation = :kuhn, vertex_density = :mass, pad, jitter, threads = true)

Density and number of streams on the regular grid xs × ys × zs directly from the particles, without constructing an estimator: the tetrahedra are unwrapped and rasterised one at a time. For the Kuhn split they are generated from the lattice on the fly, so the memory needed is that of the particles and the grid; for the Delaunay tessellation only its (T, 4) connectivity is kept. PhaseSpaceDTFE.rasterise_sb — Method
rasterise_sb(xs, ys, zs, coords_q, coords_x, m, sim_box; N_sub = 2, pad = 5, periodicity = true, threads = true,
             density_type = Float64, streams_type = Int, parallel = 1)
rasterise_sb(xs, ys, zs, ps_dtfe_sb::PS_DTFE_subbox; threads = true, density_type = Float64, streams_type = Int, parallel = 1)

Density and number of streams on the regular grid xs × ys × zs, as arrays indexed [ix, iy, iz], from the sub-box estimators: the tetrahedra of every sub-box are rasterised into the same grid (see rasterise!), holding one estimator in memory at a time. Equal at every grid point to the sum over the sub-boxes of the point queries density and numberOfStreams of the sub-box estimators.

The grid takes sizeof(density_type) + sizeof(streams_type) bytes per point; density_type = Float32 and streams_type = UInt16 (at most 65535 streams) reduce it from 16 to 6 bytes. Pass nothing for an array that is not needed. With parallel = k, k sub-box estimators are constructed (or loaded) concurrently, which needs k times the memory of one; the result does not depend on k. The same keywords apply to density_grid_sb, numberOfStreams_grid_sb and velocity_grid_sb.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.velocity_grid — Method
velocity_grid(xs, ys, zs, estimator; threads = true, velocity_type = Float64, deformation = false)

Velocities of all streams on the regular grid xs × ys × zs, as a StreamVelocityGrid G: G[ix, iy, iz] equals velocity([x, y, z], estimator) at the grid point, up to the order of the streams. With deformation = true the divergence, shear and vorticity of every stream are stored too (see stream_divergence, stream_shear, stream_vorticity, stream_average). Computed by rasterising the tetrahedra twice, to count the streams of every grid point and to fill in their values. The grid takes 12 bytes per grid point and 4 sizeof(velocity_type) bytes per stream, 14 sizeof(velocity_type) with deformation.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.velocity_grid_sb — Method
velocity_grid_sb(xs, ys, zs, coords_q, coords_x, velocities, m, sim_box; N_sub = 2, pad = 5, periodicity = true,
                 threads = true, velocity_type = Float64, deformation = false)
velocity_grid_sb(xs, ys, zs, ps_dtfe_sb::PS_DTFE_subbox; threads = true, velocity_type = Float64, deformation = false)

Velocities of all streams on the regular grid xs × ys × zs from the sub-box estimators, as a StreamVelocityGrid G: G[ix, iy, iz] holds the rows of velocity of every sub-box estimator at the grid point, up to the order of the streams. With deformation = true the divergence, shear and vorticity of every stream are stored too, as in velocity_grid. The sub-boxes are rasterised twice, holding one estimator at a time: first to count the streams of every grid point, then to fill in their velocities. On the fly, every sub-box estimator is therefore constructed twice; with the estimators saved by PS_DTFE_subbox, they are only read twice.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.CellIndex — Type
CellIndex

Uniform G³ cell index of a set of tetrahedra over the box [0, L)³, in compressed form: the tetrahedra that intersect the cell k = i + (j - 1) G + (l - 1) G² are tets[offsets[k]:offsets[k + 1] - 1], in increasing order. For a periodic index the cells are taken at any periodic image; otherwise the cells at the faces extend to infinity, so that points outside the box find the tetrahedra there.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.delaunay — Method
delaunay(coords_q; jitter = 1e-5, ids = axes(coords_q, 1), spacing = nothing)

Delaunay tessellation of the points coords_q (no periodic images), in the canonical order of canonical_tetrahedra. For degenerate configurations such as a lattice, the connectivity is computed from positions displaced by jitter mean particle spacings in a pseudo-random direction fixed by the particle id ids[r] of every row, so that every copy of a particle (in another sub-box, or a periodic image in a frame) is displaced identically and degenerate cells are split consistently. spacing is the mean particle spacing (default: from the bounding box).

PhaseSpaceDTFE.periodic_delaunay — Method
periodic_delaunay(coords_q, L; pad = nothing, jitter = 1e-5, slabs = nothing)

Delaunay tessellation of the initial particle positions in a periodic box of size L, as used by PhaseSpaceDTFE, for lattice or glass initial conditions. The positions are tetrahedralised with TetGen together with their periodic images within a frame of width pad * L around the box (by default four mean particle spacings), and of every periodic tetrahedron the copy whose centroid lies in [c₀, c₀ + L)³ is kept. The reference cell is shifted by an irrational fraction c₀ of the mean particle spacing, so that no centroid of a (degenerate) lattice tessellation lies on its boundary. For degenerate configurations such as an exact lattice, where the Delaunay tessellation is not unique, the connectivity is computed from positions displaced by a fixed pseudo-random jitter (in mean particle spacings), the same for every periodic image of a particle, so that all images are split consistently; the volumes are computed from the exact positions.

The reference cell is split along z into slabs slabs (by default one per thread, at least two frame widths thick), which are tetrahedralised concurrently: each slab with the particles and periodic images within the frame width of it, keeping the tetrahedra whose centroid lies in the slab. The Delaunay tessellation is local, so the slabs together give the tetrahedra of a single tessellation of the box; slabs = 1 uses one call of TetGen.

Returns the (T, 4) matrix of particle rows and the Lagrangian volumes of the tetrahedra, in the canonical order of canonical_tetrahedra. The kept tetrahedra must tile the box exactly; if their volumes do not add up to L³, the frame was too narrow and an error is thrown.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.raster_visit! — Method
raster_visit!(visit, xs, ys, zs, zr, o, E, B, L, coords, wrapped; images = :all)

Call visit(ix, iy, iz, λ1, λ2, λ3) at every grid point (ix, iy, iz), with z-index in zr, that lies in the tetrahedron with first corner o, edge matrix E and its inverse B, with the barycentric coordinates λ of the point. Every plane cuts the tetrahedron, at every periodic image that overlaps the grid, in a convex polygon; for every grid row the x-interval of the polygon is computed (scanline) and the grid points in it, with a tolerance of PAD grid spacings, are tested with the barycentric coordinates. coords are the grid coordinates and wrapped the same modulo L. The barycentric coordinates are computed exactly as in a point query of an estimator with the same images (see PS_DTFE), so that the result equals point queries at the grid points:

  • :all: from the separation of the wrapped coordinate from the first corner at its nearest periodic image.
  • :modulo: from the plain separation of the wrapped coordinate; the image shifted by k L along an axis is only tested at the grid points g with fld(g, L) == k, the others cannot lie in it.
  • :none: from the plain separation of the grid coordinate; only the unshifted tetrahedron.

PhaseSpaceDTFE.subbox_particles — Method

PhaseSpaceDTFE.vertex_densities_periodic — Method
vertex_densities_periodic(x, tets, VL, m, L, mode)

Density at the particles from the Eulerian volumes V of the tetrahedra around each particle (its star W):

  • mode = :mass: ρᵢ = 4 mᵢ / V(Wᵢ), as in PhaseSpaceDTFE. Valid for any particle masses: every particle's mass is shared by the tetrahedra of its star.
  • mode = :lagrangian: ρᵢ = ρ̄ VL(Wᵢ) / V(Wᵢ), with the Lagrangian star volume VL and the mean density ρ̄ = M/L³ (Feldbrugge 2024, eq. 26). It assumes a uniform initial density and is then exact, also when the stars differ in size (glass initial conditions, a Delaunay tessellation) or the masses differ with the local resolution of the initial conditions (zoom simulations).

Both conserve the total mass M, and they coincide for the Kuhn split of a lattice with equal masses. m is a single mass or a vector with the mass of every particle.
